# 🍚 Feed the Future: Support SIBBC&S Students with Daily Rice

At South India Baptist Bible College & Seminary (SIBBC&S), 475 students are answering God’s call to ministry—many from impoverished regions of Northeast India and Myanmar. Despite persecution and hardship, they have committed their lives to sharing the Gospel.

This project is a simple but powerful way to support them: by providing daily rice.

## 🌾 Why Rice?

Each day, the seminary provides three meals to every student. The cost of rice alone is:
- **2.5 bags (75 kg each)** = 187.5 kg/day
- **₹9,375/day ≈ $110.30 USD/day**
- Just **$4.65 feeds 20 students for one day**

Your gift nourishes both body and spirit. It’s not just food—it’s fuel for future pastors, evangelists, and servants of Christ.

## ✝️ About the Ministry

SIBBC&S is a ministry—not a business—committed to training Christian workers to faithfully proclaim the Gospel. Tuition is kept low to remain accessible, and students work campus jobs to help cover costs. Free housing and meals are provided, but the financial burden is growing.

This page was created to invite believers around the world to share their plate and bless those who hunger for both the Word and daily bread.
